EMBERCLASH

A duel of four elements. Outwit the Ash Warden, card by card.

Goal — reduce the Ash Warden from 30 HP to 0 before yours falls.
Play — click a card to play it (costs mana ◆), click your creature, then an enemy to attack.
Surge — play two cards of the same element in one turn: your creatures gain +1 attack.
Guard — Stone creatures with Guard must be attacked before the hero.
Keys — 1–7 play a hand card · Space ends turn · R restarts · Esc cancels.

30 HP each · 24-card decks · four elements: Ember ▲ · Tide ● · Leaf ◆ · Stone ■
